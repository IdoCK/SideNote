# SideNote copy direction

## Positioning

Capture the thought. Come back to it later.

SideNote is a frictionless place to put down a thought without breaking your flow. Jot it down in seconds and keep going. When you're ready, everything you captured is waiting in Review — organized by day and time, with checkboxes to help you work through it.

## Decision rule

The owner's core concept: “SideNote isn't where you work on thoughts. It's where you put them so you don't have to work on them right now.”

Write around the relief of putting a thought somewhere and continuing the activity it interrupted. The sequence is capture, keep going, return when ready. Review offers a calm way to work through what was captured; avoid promising a full thinking workspace, project-management system or AI organizer.

Use direct everyday verbs: capture, jot down, speak, put down, keep going, come back, review, check off. Emphasize low interruption before discussing navigation or storage. Keep Android setup requirements and simulated-demo labels accurate. Avoid romanticizing the thought itself or implying visitors must organize it during capture.

Keep the headline aligned across the project card and case page through the canonical bundle's tagline field. Review screenshots for the story they support, and record reuse when the UI has not changed.
