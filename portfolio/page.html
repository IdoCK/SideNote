The interaction

Catch it now.
Come back to it later.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Set SideNote as your digital assistant to open Capture by holding Power, or use the Capture shortcut. Speak or type, with dictation continuing across pauses.

  2. 02

    Put it down

    Placing the phone face-down or turning the screen off saves the draft.

  3. 03

    Return later

    Tap a date or browse a project. Longer notes expand in place; mark a thought processed when you are done.

Browser reconstruction of SideNote 1.0.12, using a fictional note. Phone gesture illustrated.

Capture now, organize later.

Capture has its own entry point. A consistent Dates, Projects and Settings bar keeps returning to a thought simple, without getting in the way of recording it.

Notes remain ordinary files.

Notes are stored as Markdown in a folder you choose. Project tags and processed states live in those same files, with no SideNote account or separate note database.

Inside the Android app

A quieter way to review

Large date targets make days easy to reach. Longer notes unfold where they are, while the same navigation stays in place across Review and Settings.
