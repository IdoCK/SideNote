SideNote

Capture the thought.
Come back to it later.

SideNote is a frictionless place to put down a thought without breaking your flow. Jot it down in seconds and keep going. When you’re ready, everything you captured is waiting in Review — organized by day and time, with checkboxes to help you work through it.

The interaction

Put it down & keep going

  1. 01

    Capture

    Speak or jot down what’s on your mind. Open Capture with its predefined shortcut (OS specific).

  2. 02

    Keep going

    Trigger a note save by fliping the phone face-down, or by turning the screen off. Then carry on with what you were doing.

  3. 03

    Review

    Come back to your thoughts when you’re ready. Review has all your thoughts ordered by date and time. Work through and check them off.

Browser reconstruction of SideNote 1.0.12, using a fictional note. Phone gesture illustrated.

Capture takes a moment.

A thought can arrive in the middle of something else. Give it somewhere to wait, without having to decide what to do with it before you carry on.

Notes remain ordinary files.

Notes are stored as local Markdown in a folder of your choice. Project tags and processed states live in those same files, with no SideNote account or separate note database.
