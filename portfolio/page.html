SideNote

Capture the thought.
Come back to it later.

SideNote is a frictionless place to put down a thought without breaking your flow. Jot it down in seconds and keep going. When you’re ready, everything you captured is waiting in Review — organized by day and time, with checkboxes to help you work through it.

The interaction

Put it down.
Keep going.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Speak or jot down what’s on your mind. Open Capture with its shortcut, or hold Power after setting SideNote as your digital assistant.

  2. 02

    Keep going

    Put the phone face-down or turn the screen off to save. Then carry on with what you were doing.

  3. 03

    Come back later

    When you’re ready, Review has it waiting by day and time. Expand a note to read it, then check it off as you work through what you captured.

Browser reconstruction of SideNote 1.0.12, using a fictional note. Phone gesture illustrated.

Capture takes a moment.

A thought can arrive in the middle of something else. Give it somewhere to wait, without having to decide what to do with it before you carry on.

Notes remain ordinary files.

Notes are stored as Markdown in a folder you choose. Project tags and processed states live in those same files, with no SideNote account or separate note database.

Inside the Android app

Waiting for you in Review

Come back when you have the time and attention. Your thoughts are grouped by day, each with its capture time and a checkbox. Read a little more, check one off, and move through them at your own pace.
